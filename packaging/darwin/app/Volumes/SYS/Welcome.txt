Welcome to ClassicStack!

This is the "SYS" volume, exported over NCP (Novell NetWare 3.x bindery)
for NETx/VLM DOS clients.

You can edit or remove this share, or add your own, from the web admin UI:
click "Open Interface" in the ClassicStack menu bar item.

This folder lives at:
~/Library/Application Support/ClassicStack/Volumes/SYS
