Welcome to ClassicStack!

This is the "Public" share, exported over both AFP (for classic Macs) and
SMB (for Windows/DOS clients) from the same folder on this machine — files
you drop here show up on both.

You can edit or remove this share, or add your own, from the web admin UI:
click "Open Interface" in the ClassicStack menu bar item.

This folder lives at:
~/Library/Application Support/ClassicStack/Volumes/Public
